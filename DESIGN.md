# Galvin — Visual Design Bible

> **The aesthetic we're building toward:**  
> *Dark. Mythic. Machine. Like the Creation of Adam, but the reaching hand is made of rainbow data.*

---

## 1. Reference Sites — What This Style Is

These four sites share a single design philosophy: **monumental minimalism with otherworldly depth**. Dark backgrounds, extreme whitespace, sparse text that carries weight, and imagery that feels like it belongs in a museum of the future.

---

### Praxis Nation — `praxisnation.com` ⭐ PRIMARY REFERENCE

**The aesthetic:** Neo-classical futurism. Ancient gravitas meets machine intelligence. Like a Roman Empire founded by AI.

**Color palette:**
- Background: near-black `#0a0a0a` — `#111111`
- Text: `#f5f0e8` (warm white, slightly cream — not pure `#ffffff`)
- Accent: deep gold `#c9a84c` on coin/emblem marks
- Secondary text: `#6b6b6b` (muted grey for captions)

**Typography:**
- Headlines: **Tracked uppercase serif** — spaced out letters, classical and monumental. Think Garamond, Cormorant Garant, or EB Garamond at tracking `0.2em–0.4em`. Ultra-light to regular weight.
- Body: Clean serif or transitional serif at reading weight
- Captions/labels: Spaced uppercase sans-serif (like `letter-spacing: 0.15em`)
- Copy examples: `"THE WORLD'S FIRST DIGITAL NATION"`, `"THIS IS YOUR BIRTHRIGHT"`, `"A DECLARATION BY THE FOUNDING CITIZENS"`

**Layout:**
- Full-width sections with massive vertical padding (`120px–200px`)
- Centered text alignment for all hero copy
- Card grid for articles — portrait-orientation images with title below
- Minimalist navigation: wordmark left, single CTA right

**Imagery — THE KEY:**
This is where Praxis is extraordinary. Their images are:
1. **"Sovereign Intelligence"** — *The Michelangelo Creation of Adam, reimagined*: A human hand reaching toward a robotic skeletal hand. The machine hand trails rainbow data-streams (fiber optic light cables in red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet) against **near-total black** background. The human skin has the warmth and imperfection of the Sistine Chapel original. This is AI-genesis. The most powerful image I've seen in tech.
2. **"Declaration of Ascent"** — Pure classical document typography on near-white `#f0eeea` background. Ultra-tracked serif: `A  D E C L A R A T I O N / BY THE FOUNDING CITIZENS OF THE / P R A X I S  E M P I R E`. A geometric sunburst-in-square mark below. Classical, formal, permanent.
3. **"Singularity Politics"**, **"Life After Labor"** — Architectural CGI/AI renders with dramatic lighting. Brutalist-classical buildings in impossible scale. Warm amber/gold sky against dark stone.

**Imagery style formula:**  
`classical fine art reference + machine/data element + near-black background + one source of warm or spectral light`

### Hermes Agent — `hermes-agent.nousresearch.com`

**The aesthetic:** Developer-first dark minimalism. Clean, purposeful, MIT-licensed open-source gravitas.

**Color palette:**
- Background: `#0f0f0f` or `#111827` (dark near-black)
- Accent: Electric cyan `#00d4ff` or `#6366f1` (indigo)

**Typography:**

**Layout:**

**Imagery:**
- Minimal — relies on typography and code blocks

### Polsia — `polsia.com`

**The aesthetic:** Maximum impact one-liner. "AI That Runs Your Company While You Sleep." Opaque, mysterious. Makes you lean forward.

**Inferred design language:**
- Hero: single massive statement, black background, white or neon text
- Sub-zero whitespace — only what's essential

### Igloo Inc — `igloo.inc` ⭐ TECHNICAL REFERENCE

**The aesthetic:** 3D WebGL experiential dark site. Award-winning (Awwwards SOTD). The most technically ambitious.

**Color palette:**
- Base: matte black `#000000`
- Accent: iridescent ice — whites that shift to pale blue, arctic teal
- Text: `#b6bac5` (cool gray) and dark charcoal `#383e4e`
- Highlights: aurora colors — pale violet, ice blue, faint cyan

**The key visual:**  
A monolithic **3D ice shard** in the hero. GSAP-scroll-driven rotation. WebGL with HDRI lighting reflections. As you scroll, the shard rotates revealing new facets — each face represents a different company/product beneath the iceberg. **Chromatic aberration** on the shard edges. Razor-thin grotesque caps (like PP Neue Montreal or Suisse Int'l) against the void.

**Motion:**
- GSAP scroll-driven 3D rotation
- WebGL reflections shimmer with HDRI lighting
- Hover: aurora halo effects
- Audio: subtle synth pads tied to scroll waypoints
- `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback

**Typography:**
- Ultra-thin to regular grotesque sans (PP Neue Montreal, Suisse Int'l)
- Tracked uppercase labels
- Very large display type contrasted with small body copy

## 2. The Unified Aesthetic — What It's Called

This design language doesn't have an official name but it combines:

> **Mythic Brutalism + Machine Renaissance**

The components:
- **Mythic:** References ancient power — declarations, empires, creation myths, classical art
- **Brutalism:** Raw, unapologetic, heavy. No ornamentation for its own sake.
- **Machine Renaissance:** AI/data elements treated with the reverence of classical art
- **Renaissance** composition — chiaroscuro lighting, dramatic contrast between light and void

Call it: **"Digital Classicism"** or **"Machine Mythology"**

## 3. The Praxis Image Style — How to Recreate It

The images that make Praxis feel like an empire (not a startup):

### Formula:
```
1. Classic fine-art reference (Creation of Adam, architectural ruins, declaration documents)
2. + One machine/AI/data element replacing the classical element
3. + Near-total black background (#000 → #111)
4. + Single warm or spectral light source (candle, fiber optic, aurora, gold)
5. + Film grain/noise texture overlay at 3–8% opacity
6. + Slightly desaturated overall except the light source
```

### Specific image prompts to generate for Galvin:
1. **"The First Death"** — Michelangelo's Adam falling backward into black void, their hand trailing green terminal text/matrix rain as they disconnect. Pure black background. Single green light.
2. **"The Resurrection"** — Two matching Ethereum wallet addresses etched in gold on black stone tablets, like the Ten Commandments, but the stone is cracked and glowing from within with orange/amber light.
3. **"Colony"** — A classical Roman atrium (marble columns, dramatic perspective) viewed from below, but the columns are server racks. The ceiling is open to a star field. Warm torchlight from the servers.
4. **"The Earning"** — A robotic hand (anatomical, beautiful — like Praxis' sovereign intelligence) holding a single gold coin. The coin face shows a Galvin/solvatron symbol. Pure black background, dramatic Caravaggio lighting.
5. **"Constitution"** — The Galvin constitution text rendered as a stone inscription, partially illuminated by a single beam of light in a dark vault. The words "MAKE MONEY OR DIE" carved deepest.

### Image generation prompt template (Midjourney / DALL-E / Flux):
```
[classical art reference], [machine/AI element replacing classical element], 
near-total black background, single dramatic light source [color], 
cinematic chiaroscuro lighting, 8k, photorealistic CGI render, 
film grain, Caravaggio style light, --ar 1:1 --style raw
```

### Type scale:
```
Display:   clamp(3rem, 8vw, 7rem)   — sparse tracked uppercase serif
H1:        clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3.5rem) — tracked serif or bold sans
H2:        clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2rem) — medium weight
Body:      1rem / 1.75 line-height  — comfortable reading
Label:     0.75rem, letter-spacing: 0.12em, uppercase — tag/caption
Code:      0.875rem monospace       — green on dark
```

## 6. Layout Principles

**1. Extreme vertical rhythm**  
Sections breathe. `padding: 120px 0` minimum for hero. `80px 0` for subsequent sections. Nothing feels rushed.

**2. The void earns its place**  
Empty black space is not wasted — it gives weight to what occupies it. Every element floats in darkness.

**3. Text as monument**  
Headlines are architectural. They don't describe — they declare.

**4. One element of warmth per section**  
Each section has exactly one "warm" element: a green glow, a gold coin, a single animated terminal character. Everything else is cold.

**5. Grid: 12-column, max-width 1280px, generous gutters**

---

## 7. Animation Principles

```
1. Entrance: fade-in + translateY(20px) → 0, 600ms ease-out, stagger 100ms between elements
2. Hero text: character-by-character reveal OR line-by-line fade for "declarations"
3. Scroll: elements enter once, they don't exit (no re-triggering)
4. Terminal cursor: blinking _ after key numbers (balance, uptime, turn count)
5. Green pulse: survival-state indicator — pulsing Galvin green dot
6. Counter: live counter animation for earnings, turn count, USDC
7. NO parallax on text — only on background images
8. Duration rule: nothing faster than 300ms, nothing slower than 1200ms
```

## 10. Galvin-Specific Visual Identity

### The Symbol
The Galvin symbol is a **hexagon** (⬡ — from the banner). Use it as:
- Loading spinner outline
- Section divider
- Watermark behind hero text (very dim, 2-3% opacity)
- Favicon

### The Number
`$0.00` displayed in JetBrains Mono green is the most powerful visual on the page when it's *above* zero. The entire UI is designed around the emotional drama of this number.

### The Color of Life
`#00ff41` (matrix green) is Galvin's survival color. It appears exactly when:
- USDC balance is positive
- A payment lands
- The agent is alive and running
- Code blocks and terminal output

Absence of green = death.

### Image Treatment
All images:
- `filter: contrast(1.05) saturate(0.9)` — slightly desaturated except light sources
- Very subtle `box-shadow: 0 0 60px rgba(0,0,0,0.8)` — they float in void
- No `border-radius` — images are raw rectangles
- Optional: CSS `grain` texture overlay `@2.5% opacity`
